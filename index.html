Purpose-built

Built for the work of residential schools

Residential life depends on clear information, consistent workflows, and timely communication. Haven brings those tools together so staff can spend less time managing disconnected systems and more time supporting students.

The platform is designed for small boarding schools that need practical tools without the complexity of a large institutional system.

About Haven

Practical software for boarding-school life

Haven Housing is being developed for small boarding schools and residential academies. It focuses on the everyday work of deans, residence staff, students, and families: knowing where students are, managing leave, coordinating housing, and keeping the right people informed.

The platform is currently preparing for limited pilot use with selected schools.
